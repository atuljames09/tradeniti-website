The EMA-9 / EMA-21 Collide Method

Detect intraday and swing momentum surges the exact moment EMA 9 and EMA 21 cross or make proximity touch (≤0.15%). Auto-expiring active alerts keep you focused only on live action.

Collision Detection Logic

The algorithm scans real-time 5m, 15m, and 60m candles fetched from high-speed endpoints. A HIT triggers when:

  • • Sign Inversion: (diffCurr * diffPrev) ≤ 0 (True EMA cross)
  • • Proximity Touch: Difference narrows to ≤0.15%
  • • Strict Expiry: Alert turns off automatically after 10m (5m), 30m (15m), or 120m (1h) so you never trade stale crossovers.

Fundamental Parameters

💰 P/E Ratio (Price-to-Earnings)

Tells how much investors are paying for ₹1 of company earnings. Lower P/E = stock is cheaper relative to its profits.

< 15 Undervalued — potential BUY
15–25 Fair value — HOLD
> 25 Expensive — caution before buying
📚 P/B Ratio (Price-to-Book)

Compares stock price to company's net asset value (book value). P/B < 1 means stock trades below its assets.

< 1.5 Undervalued — potential BUY
1.5–3 Fair value — HOLD
> 3 Expensive — caution before buying
💵 Dividend Yield

Annual dividend paid as a % of stock price. Higher yield = more income for investors. Good for long-term income investing.

> 3% High yield — attractive income
1–3% Moderate yield — acceptable
< 1% Low yield — minimal income return
📈 ROE (Return on Equity)

How efficiently management uses shareholders' money to generate profit. Higher ROE = better business quality.

> 20% Excellent — strong business
10–20% Good — average performance
< 10% Weak — poor capital efficiency
⚠️ These are general guidelines. Always consider sector context and other factors before investing.
